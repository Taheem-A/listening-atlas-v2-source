What these numbers mean

Listening time includes all valid music events in the selected dates. Play counts use your chosen minimum duration. Skip rate uses only events with a known skip flag. Dates use the timezone you choose and the export’s playback end timestamps.

Durations are not in the history export. Completion uses known durations only: mean and median are event-weighted; full listens are ≥90%, near-complete listens ≥75% (including full listens), and early exits <25%. Unknown durations never count as 0%. Artwork, likes, genres and playlist membership remain unavailable. Artist totals use the export’s album-artist name; album identity uses artist + album name.

Duration metadata

Spotify enrichment is optional. History and existing statistics work without it.

Spotify’s current developer policy restricts derived listenership analytics. API enrichment requires permission covering this use. Spotify-derived completion is excluded from ChatGPT profiles. Read Spotify’s policy.

Import independently sourced durations

Import a JSON array of {"spotify_track_uri":"spotify:track:…","duration_ms":200000} to add completion statistics by exact track ID. Existing duration files remain supported; their provenance is unknown until you provide a source. No title matching is performed.
